01 / The Mission

Making it natural for an Indian student with an idea to seek funding.

In India, millions of sharp ideas stay buried in notebooks, exam answer sheets, and dorm room conversations. The conventional path urges students to pass exams and hunt for traditional corporate jobs, offering almost no early runway to prototype, experiment, or build.

Lineup exists to fundamentally rewrite that instinct:

“A student gets an idea in a classroom, and the next thing that comes to his mind is — I can try to fund this with Lineup.”
— The Lineup Dream
02 / The Shift

Built for the realities of first-time young founders.

Traditional finance is built for established corporations and serial entrepreneurs with balance sheets. Lineup is architected from day zero for students.

The Traditional Route
The Lineup Approach
Requires formal business setup

Needs registered company paperwork, CA audits, tax accounts, and upfront legal fees before testing an idea.

Launch as a student or individual

Start with a concept, research project, or hardware prototype. No incorporation needed to seek validation.

Pitching closed investor rooms

Venture funds look for established revenue and full-time dedication, shutting out young students.

Direct public community backing

Get small and meaningful contributions directly from peers, mentors, and believers who care about your cause.

Transactional and isolated

Donation platforms treat backers like invoice receipts. Once money is collected, the relationship ends.

Continuous community via Lineup Socials

Supporters follow creator logs, discuss breakthroughs, test prototypes, and remain engaged for the long run.

08 / Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lineup?

Lineup is India’s first youth-focused crowdfunding platform designed for students, teens, and young adults (ages 16–30) to raise funding and build early community for their ideas and prototypes.

Do I need a registered company to raise funds on Lineup?

No. Unlike traditional VC or business financing, Lineup allows young innovators to start with early-stage prototypes, concepts, and student projects without requiring incorporation, CA audits, or corporate overhead.

What is Lineup Socials?

Lineup Socials is a built-in community feature that allows creators to share transparent progress logs, celebrate milestones, and interact with backer communities long after campaign launch.

Who founded Lineup?

Lineup was created by a team of teenagers (ages 16 and younger) led by Mohammed Asif (also known as Leader Asif).

What is the mission behind Lineup?

The mission of Lineup is "For the Idea Nation™" — to make it normal for any Indian student with an idea in a classroom to think: "I can try to fund this idea with Lineup."
